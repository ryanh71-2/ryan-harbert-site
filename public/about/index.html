About me

Ryan Harbert

I’m Ryan. I offer Focusing sessions online and write about the practice here.

My interest in this work comes from my own experience of healing and from the people who have helped me along the way. Meditation, spiritual practice, and Focusing have all been part of that.

I began practicing Focusing in 2009. What has kept me with it is its capacity for deep emotional healing. I’ve found that listening to how a difficulty feels in my body can bring me into contact with something that thinking about it hasn’t reached. This is a practice I continue to learn from, and one I teach and guide.

I trained for two years and became a Certified Focusing Professional through the International Focusing Institute in 2020. I’ve taught individuals and small groups, guided private sessions, and completed advanced training in Wholebody Focusing. I also trained and certified as a Progressive Recovery Coach in 2018.

Outside sessions, I’m married and have three children. We’ve spent much of our life together traveling and living in other countries. I appreciate the slower pace we’ve found in many places, and the chance to learn from people who live differently. I studied philosophy and religious studies, and those interests are still very much alive for me. When I can, I hike, climb mountains, and make art.

I am a Focusing practitioner, not a psychotherapist. This work can sit alongside therapy or other forms of support.
