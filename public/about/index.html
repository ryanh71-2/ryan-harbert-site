About me

Ryan Harbert

I’m Ryan. I offer Focusing and deep listening sessions for men who are struggling with something they haven’t been able to change or resolve.

My interest in this work comes out of my own life. I struggled with pornography addiction for more than 25 years. Along the way, I spent years in meditation, spiritual practice, and recovery groups, learning from people whose understanding and support made a difference to me.

I began practicing Focusing in 2009. What has kept me with it is its capacity for deep emotional healing. By listening closely to how we carry a difficulty in our bodies, we can begin to work with parts of our experience that thinking and talking alone may not reach. Longstanding emotional patterns can shift, and places where we’ve felt stuck can open into new understanding and movement. My part is to help you find and stay with that process, at a pace that feels right for you.

I trained for two years and became a Certified Focusing Professional through the International Focusing Institute in 2020. I’ve taught individuals and small groups, guided private sessions, and completed advanced training in Wholebody Focusing. I also trained and certified as a Progressive Recovery Coach in 2018.

Outside sessions, I’m married and have three children. We’ve spent much of our life together traveling and living in other countries. I appreciate the slower pace we’ve found in many places, and the chance to learn from people who live differently. I studied philosophy and religious studies, and those interests are still very much alive for me. When I can, I hike, climb mountains, and make art.

I am a Focusing practitioner, not a psychotherapist. This work can sit alongside therapy or other forms of support.
