Sessions

I offer guided Focusing sessions for men working with compulsive behaviors. These might involve pornography, internet use, eating, or other patterns you’ve had difficulty changing.

You may have tried several ways to change and still find yourself struggling. We can begin there. You don’t need to know Focusing or have a clear explanation of what is happening.

In a session

What we do in a session

We begin with something you’re having difficulty with. It might be an urge, something that happened during the week, or a feeling you’ve been trying to get away from.

I help you notice how it feels in your body and find a way to stay with it without becoming overwhelmed. I may suggest a pause, reflect something you’ve said, or invite you to check whether a word really fits what you’re feeling. You decide whether a suggestion helps.

This is how you begin learning Focusing through your own experience. We give attention to what you find, including what is painful or doesn’t yet make sense. You can keep the details of your story private.

Over time, you can develop this skill to use on your own or with a Focusing partner.

Practical details

My standard rate is $95 USD. I offer a limited number of reduced-fee places at $60 or $30 per session for people who cannot comfortably afford the standard rate. Please ask about availability when you get in touch.

There is no package or long-term commitment. Focusing can be used alongside psychotherapy, recovery programs, or other forms of care.

These sessions are not psychotherapy or crisis care. If you need immediate help or emergency support, contact local emergency services or a crisis service where you are.

Common questions

Before we meet

What happens in a session?

We begin with what you’d like to explore. I help you slow down and notice how it feels in you, even if you don’t yet have words for it. I may offer a suggestion or reflect what I hear; you decide whether it fits. There’s no need to arrive knowing how to Focus.

Do I have to tell you the details?

No. You can share as much or as little of the story as you wish. Often we can work with how something feels to you without going through everything that happened.

What can I bring?

A difficult relationship, anxiety, a compulsive pattern, a decision, or simply a vague sense that something is off. You don’t need to sort it out before the session. We can begin with what you notice.

How is this different from therapy?

These are guided Focusing sessions, not psychotherapy. I don’t diagnose you or analyze your history. We work with your present experience and help you develop a way of listening to yourself. Many people use Focusing alongside therapy or other care.

Will what I say be private?

I treat what you share in a session as private. You choose what to tell me, and you need not name other people or recount events. If you have questions about confidentiality or meeting online, please ask before we begin.
