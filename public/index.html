I’m Ryan. I offer one-to-one Focusing sessions online. I help people slow down and listen to what they’re experiencing, especially when something is difficult or unclear.

I also write here about Focusing and what I’m learning through practicing it.

About the sessions

How I work

Focusing is a skill of listening to yourself. It involves attending to how something feels in your body, including what you haven’t yet been able to put into words.

I’ve practiced Focusing since 2009. It has helped me heal, and I value being able to share it with others. In a session, I offer guidance as you learn to notice and stay with your own experience. You decide whether a suggestion fits and how far you want to go.
