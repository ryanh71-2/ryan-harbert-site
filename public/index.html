Focusing & deep listening

I work online with men who are struggling with something they haven’t been able to change or resolve. This might involve relationships, anxiety, depression, porn or other compulsive patterns, or a sense of being stuck.

I’m especially interested in working with people who have already tried quite a lot.

About working together ↗

How I work

My part is to listen closely and help you stay with what needs your attention, at a pace that feels workable.

Focusing begins with the felt, bodily sense of a situation, including what we do not yet have words for. We slow down and give that sense a little room.

Working together

We meet online. The first session is free, so we can find out whether this way of working feels useful to you. After that, you choose your fee from suggested amounts.
